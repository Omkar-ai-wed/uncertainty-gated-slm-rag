### Step 1: Manuscript Formatting & Content Rules
- [ ] **Word Processor:** Manuscript prepared in Microsoft Word (.docx) format using the official AAIR template.
- [ ] **Typography:** All headings and body text formatted in **Cambria** font (Main text 9 pt, justified, two-column layout with 85 mm width and 10 mm gutter).
- [ ] **Title:** 14 pt Cambria centered. English title provided (Turkish translation included per template).
- [ ] **Abstract:** Fully justified, 8 pt italics, strictly $\le 300$ words (Current draft: 218 words).
- [ ] **Keywords:** Exactly 3–6 keywords provided immediately following the abstract.
- [ ] **Page Limit:** Strictly $\le 15$ pages including all figures, tables, and references (Current draft: ~12 pages).
- [ ] **Figures & Tables Limit:** Combined total $\le 10$ display items (Current count: 2 Figures, 2 Tables = 4 items). All figures minimum 300 DPI resolution.
- [ ] **Citation Style:** Strictly IEEE numeric format (`[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]`) ordered sequentially in order of appearance. Total references $< 60$ (Current count: 15 primary verified references).

---

### Step 2: Anonymity & Double-Blind Peer Review Compliance
- [ ] **Blinded Manuscript File (`Manuscript_Blinded.docx`):**
  - No author names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  - No institutional affiliations, department names, or university logos.
  - No direct acknowledgment of specific lab grants that reveal the institution.
  - Citations to authors' own prior work phrased in third person (e.g., "In prior work, Doe et al. showed..." rather than "In our prior work...").
- [ ] **Unblinded Title Page File (`TitlePage.docx`):**
  - Contains complete paper title, full author names, institutional affiliations, postal addresses, ORCID iDs, and corresponding author email.

---

### Step 3: Plagiarism & Similarity Report
- [ ] **Scan Tool:** Scanned using iThenticate, Turnitin, or institutional equivalent.
- [ ] **Threshold:** Similarity score strictly $< 20\%$ (excluding bibliography and small matches $< 5$ words).
- [ ] **Upload File:** Full similarity report exported as `SimilarityReport.pdf` ready for attachment.

---

### Step 4: Cover Letter & Reviewer Nominations
- [ ] **Addressed to Editor:** Addressed to Dr. Osman Özkaraca and the Editorial Board of AAIR.
- [ ] **Novelty & Fit:** Highlights the core contribution (Uncertainty-Gated RAG in SLMs) and fit for AAIR.
- [ ] **Three Suggested Reviewers:** Full names, verified institutional emails, affiliations, and subject-matter expertise included (Drafted in `cover_letter.md`).
- [ ] **Originality & Duplicate Submission Statement:** Explicit declaration that the paper is original and not under simultaneous consideration elsewhere.
- [ ] **Conflict of Interest Declaration:** Stated clearly in both cover letter and manuscript text.

### Step 5: DergiPark Portal Submission Workflow
1. **Login:** Log in to https://dergipark.org.tr with your ORCID credentials.
2. **Navigate:** Search for `Advances in Artificial Intelligence Research` (e-ISSN: 2757-7422).
3. **Initiate:** Click "Submit a Manuscript".
4. **Metadata Entry:** Paste English/Turkish titles, abstract, and keywords into portal fields.
5. **Upload Required Documents:**
   * `Manuscript.docx` (Anonymized)
   * `TitlePage.docx` (Author details)
   * `CoverLetter.pdf` / `.docx`
   * `SimilarityReport.pdf` (Turnitin/iThenticate)
   * `CopyrightForm.pdf` (Signed AAIR copyright transfer form downloaded from DergiPark)
6. **Enter Reviewer Details:** Fill in the 3 nominated peer reviewers in the metadata form.
7. **Verify & Finalize:** Review generated PDF proof, check column alignment, and click Submit.
